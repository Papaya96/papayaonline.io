Stay Safe

Protect Yourself From Job Scams

Be cautious when an opportunity promises guaranteed income, asks for unusual upfront payments, requests passwords or one-time codes, or pressures you to act immediately.

  • Verify the company or platform independently.
  • Never share passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes.
  • Understand fees and withdrawal conditions before paying.
  • Keep records of applications, payments and communications.
Important: PapayaOnline does not guarantee employment, income, acceptance, availability or payment from any third-party platform. Opportunities, eligibility, fees and terms can change. Make your own checks before registering, applying or paying money.
