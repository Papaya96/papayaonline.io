Before You Apply

  • Read the platform's current terms, fees and payment rules.
  • Check that the job description and client information are clear.
  • Never share passwords, PINs, one-time passwords or other security codes.
  • Be cautious of requests for upfront payments, unusual fees or sensitive personal information.
  • Use the platform's official website or app rather than links from unknown sources.
