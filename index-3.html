NEW APPS & SCAM AWARENESS

Learn how to identify suspicious offers, protect your money and personal information, and know what to do if you think you have been scammed.

Stop. Check. Protect.

Before joining a new earning app or sending money, take a moment to verify the offer. A professional-looking website, social-media account or message does not automatically mean an opportunity is legitimate.

1. How to spot a scam

  • Promises of guaranteed or unusually high income with little effort.
  • Pressure to pay a registration, withdrawal, training or activation fee.
  • Requests for your password, PIN, one-time code or banking details.
  • Urgent messages such as “act now” or “your account will be closed”.
  • Payment or investment requests through unusual or untraceable methods.
  • Links, emails or app names that do not match the official company.

2. How to prevent scams

  • Download apps from official app stores and check the developer name.
  • Visit the company's official website yourself instead of trusting a random link.
  • Search for independent reviews and complaints before signing up.
  • Use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or recovery codes with anyone.
  • Do not pay money just because someone promises guaranteed earnings.

3. Before you send money

Ask yourself:

  • Who exactly am I paying?
  • Why is payment required?
  • Can I verify the company independently?
  • Is there a clear refund or cancellation policy?

If you cannot verify the answers, do not pay.

4. If you think you were scammed

  1. Stop contact: Do not send more money or codes.
  2. Secure accounts: Change passwords and enable 2FA.
  3. Contact your bank/payment provider: Report the transaction immediately and ask what recovery options are available.
  4. Save evidence: Keep receipts, usernames, phone numbers, emails, screenshots and transaction references.
  5. Report it: Report the account, website or app through the relevant platform and appropriate authorities in your country.
  6. Warn others: Share accurate information without posting private details or accusing someone without evidence.

Red Flags to Remember

Guaranteed profits Pay first to withdraw Requests for OTP/PIN Fake urgency Unverified links
